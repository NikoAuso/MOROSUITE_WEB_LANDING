# Code of Conduct

Be kind, be professional, assume good faith. Harassment, discrimination and personal attacks are
not tolerated, in issues, PRs, reviews or any other project space.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, open a private report through the repository's **Security** tab
(**Report a vulnerability**), or contact the maintainers at the address this fork publishes in its
README. All reports will be reviewed and handled confidentially.
